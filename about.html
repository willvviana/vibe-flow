How it works

From idea to track in three steps

Say what you want to hear. Vibe Flow handles prompting, generation and export.

  1. 01

    Describe your idea

    Type a mood or scene, like “rainy-day lo-fi”, and pick genres and instruments. Or upload a clip to continue.

  2. 02

    Pick your sound

    Set tempo, key, duration and format. Leave the seed empty for a surprise, or fix it to reproduce a result.

  3. 03

    Generate & download

    Watch real progress while it generates, then play it, export it and find it in your library.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How does text to music work?

Describe the genre, mood, instruments and tempo in plain language. Vibe Flow turns your choices into a prompt for MusicGen, an open-source music model that runs on your own computer, and returns an instrumental track. On a CPU, expect a few times the track length to generate.

Can I turn my own lyrics into a song?

Not yet. The local models installed today (MusicGen) make instrumental music only, with no vocals or lyrics. Vocal models such as ACE-Step and YuE need a powerful NVIDIA GPU, so they aren't enabled on this machine.

Can I start from my own audio?

Yes. Upload a WAV, FLAC, MP3 or OGG file and Vibe Flow can continue it, or use its melody as a guide (the Melody model is a separate ~5.8 GB download).

What export formats are available?

Every track is saved as a 32 kHz mono WAV, and you can export FLAC or MP3 copies. The original WAV is never altered.

Can I use the music commercially?

Not with the current models. MusicGen's weights are licensed CC-BY-NC 4.0, which is non-commercial. Check each model's licence in the Models panel before publishing anything.

Do I need musical experience?

No. If you can describe a track in a sentence, you can make one.

How long does a track take?

It depends on your hardware. On an 8-core CPU, a 4-second clip took about 35 seconds after the model loaded. Longer tracks are built in 20-second steps.

Is it free?

Yes. It runs locally, with no credits, accounts or cloud calls. You only need disk space for the model (about 2.2 GB) and a few GB of free RAM.

Which settings does the model actually follow?

Duration, seed and output format are exact. Genre, instruments, mood, BPM, key and time signature are sent to the model as text, so it follows them approximately, and each track's details show which were approximate.
